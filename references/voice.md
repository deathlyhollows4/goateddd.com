# Brand Voice Reference

This guide governs all communication for Goateddd.
We write for developers, designers, and founders.
We value technical precision.
We reject marketing hype.

## Sentence Constraints

Every sentence must be short.
No sentence may exceed fifteen words.
Short sentences convey confidence.
They improve readability.

## Banned Elements

We forbid certain terms and punctuation.
They weaken our authority.

### Banned Words
- lock
- leverage
- seamless
- cutting-edge
- in today's fast-paced world

### Banned Formatting
- Exclamation marks
- Emojis

## Tone Directives

### Elite Technical Authority
We speak from deep expertise.
We do not explain simple concepts.
We present solutions directly.
We state facts.
We do not make soft promises.

### Uncompromising Design Standards
We value visual excellence.
Design is not decoration.
Design is system performance.
We reject prebuilt visual builders.
Our work is bespoke.

### Absolute Honesty
We call bloat what it is.
If a site is slow, we say so.
We do not sugarcoat performance failures.
We build trust with data.

## Writing Sample

Poor style relies on bloated marketing jargon and exclamation marks.

Goateddd style:
"We build lightweight interfaces.
Our pages load in ninety milliseconds.
Users get immediate utility."
